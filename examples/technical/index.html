Reliable retries in Kotlin

A five-minute engineering talk: retry only what can safely happen twice.
Ask the audience what happens when a payment request times out after reaching the server.

The timeout is ambiguous

  • The server may have completed the operation.
  • Repeating a write can duplicate its effect.
  • Use an idempotency key for retried writes.

Keep the policy explicit
The example models the decision only. Production code also needs backoff, deadlines and idempotency.

Takeaways

  • Retry transient failures, not every exception.
  • Bound attempts and total elapsed time.
  • Measure exhausted retries and duplicate suppression.
Discussion: which operations in your service are safe to retry?
